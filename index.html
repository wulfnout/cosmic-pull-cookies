Questions

Frequently asked.

What makes Cosmic Pull Cookies different?

Every cookie is made by hand from good ingredients in small batches, so each one comes out with the right texture and flavour. The swirls are piped one at a time.

Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes. We keep a rotating selection of vegan and gluten-free cookies. Ask when you order and we'll tell you what's in the oven that week.

Are the kids’ cookies smaller?

Yes. The Little Astronauts cookies are a bit smaller and softer than our signatures, so they’re easier for little hands. Wormhole and Atomic Chip are the sweetest; Earth is the gentlest pick.

How far ahead should I order?

At least 24 hours for regular orders. For catering, give us 3–5 business days.

Do you ship internationally?

Not yet. We ship anywhere in the United States, and we hope to go further soon.

Can I customise my order?

Absolutely. Email custom@cosmicpullcookies.com about special flavours, colours or decorations.
